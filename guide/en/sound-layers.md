# Sound layers

Put sound on an SE row and time the drawings against its waveform.

![](img/sound.png)

- Pick a sound file with **Project** (folder) at the top left → **Open File…**, and the **Import** window puts it on an SE row.
- The waveform always shows faintly on the SE block. Open the row with **▸** at its left: on the **Audio** lane you slide the sound inside the block, or drag its edges to fade it.
- The mic button beside the play button records your voice at the playhead.
- An SE block's name and dialogue go in through the [Edit button](/en/timeline?id=the-edit-button), and are written onto the [timesheet and conte sheet](/en/sheets) as they are.
- The name also shows on the canvas as a name tag. The name tag keeps its place inside the camera frame and, like a layer, takes the SE row's **Name Tag** lane (size, letter spacing, bold and so on), **Transform**, blend and **Effects**.
