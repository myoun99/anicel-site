- **Colour wheel** — the ring picks the hue, the triangle saturation and brightness. The **recent** 10 colors and the **default palette** sit under it.
- Press the hex or R · G · B number on the bottom row to type it.
- **Palette** — press a slot for its color; **press an empty slot and the current color goes into it**. Drag a slot to move it. **−** removes the current colour; **⋯** holds **Make default** (the palette under the wheel and RGB tabs) · **Duplicate** · **Delete**.
- **Recent** — the colors your strokes used, newest first.

## Picking up a color from elsewhere

- Use the [Eyedropper](/en/tools?id=eyedropper) <span data-k="tool-eyedropper" data-k-paren></span>, or hold <kbd class="k-pc">Alt</kbd><kbd class="k-mac">⌥</kbd> while drawing and press the canvas.
- The pen's side button and the right mouse button are the eyedropper by default too (to change that: [Preferences](/en/preferences) → **Input**).

## Changing the colors of drawings you already made

Use **Color Edit** in the timeline's Shared pill to change the drawings of the selected range all at once. → [Timeline](/en/timeline)

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Replace Color | Turns the drawing's colors into the current color (transparency stays — for changing line color) |
| Delete Color · Keep Color | Erases only the parts in the current color, or erases everything but the current color (with a tolerance) |
| Clear Pixels | Empties the drawing |
| Binarize | Removes semi-transparent edges — at or above the threshold becomes opaque, below it transparent (colors stay) |
| Cut Pixels · Copy Pixels · Paste Pixels Above · Paste Pixels Below | Moving pixels drawing by drawing |
