# Timesheet & conte sheet

Every edit shows at once, and you see — live — exactly what will be printed.

![](img/timesheet.png)

![](img/conte-paper.png)

- Pick **Timesheet**, **Conte Sheet** or **Cut envelope** from the tabs at the top of the panel on the right. Turn pages on the strip at the bottom of the panel.
- Turn on the brush button (**Allow Brush**) at the top of the sheet and draw straight on it, as on paper. Draw in a cell's picture on the conte sheet and it becomes that cut's conte drawing. The brush, eraser and shape tools draw; with any other tool, a press moves the sheet.
- Change a drawing, a line of dialogue or the camera on the timeline, and the sheet is written on the spot. Rows whose [colour label](/en/layer-labels) is inbetween go into the CELL column; other drawing rows go into the ACTION column.
- The CAM column gets the camera keys' names and moves (PAN, T.U, T.B).
- Press the name, episode, title and cut fields at the head of the timesheet to edit them in place.
- Drag an image from the [media pool](/en/media-pool) onto a cell of the sheet and it goes in as a photo. With **Allow Brush** on, grab a photo to move it and change its size and rotation.
- The conte sheet's cover and blank pages are added and removed in **Conte sheet format**.
- It [exports](/en/export) as you see it.
