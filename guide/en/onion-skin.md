# Onion skin

Draw while seeing the drawings before and after laid faintly over this one. It is turned on **per layer** (there is no global switch).

| Where | |
|---|---|
| ![](img/icon-rail-onion-skin-button.png ':class=icon :no-zoom') Top of the tool strip | The current layer<span data-k="onion-skin-toggle" data-k-paren></span> |
| The **Onion skin** cell on a layer row | That layer. Press and sweep up or down for several rows at once |
| The onion skin cell on the rail's head row | **Apply onion skin to displayed layers / Clear onion skin on displayed layers** |

It works on animation, storyboard and direction layers.

## The onion skin panel

| | |
|---|---|
| ![](img/icon-rail-group-rail-R4.png ':class=icon :no-zoom') | The button on the right strip (shared with Tool size). What you set here is an app setting, the same for every project |

![](img/panel-onion-skin.png ':class=panel')

- The ● in the middle is the current drawing; the 8 on the left come **before**, the 8 on the right **after**.
- **The height of a bar = its strength**. Drag it up or down (they start at 40% before, 30% after).
- Press the number under a bar to turn just that one off; press again to bring its strength back.

| Buttons at the top | What they pick |
|---|---|
| **Blocks** / **Frames** | Whether a peg is one drawing or one frame |
| **Colors** / **Images** | Ghosts in set colors / in the drawings' own colors |
| **In place** (the default) / **On top** | Tucked right under each layer's drawing (covered by the layers above, following that layer's blend, folder and clipping) / above every layer |
| Red · green circles | The colors for the drawings before and after, with Colors |

## To see another layer through

Onion skin shows the drawings before and after on that layer itself. To use another layer's drawing as an underdrawing, keep that layer on and lower its **opacity**.
