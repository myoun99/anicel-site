# Shortcuts

These are the default keys. Opened on a Mac or an iPad, the page shows the Mac spelling.

## Changing shortcuts

See and change the key of every action in **Settings** (⚙) → **Keyboard shortcuts**.

- **Presets**: pick **Anicel** · **Clip Studio based** · **TVPaint based**, or make your own (New preset · Duplicate preset · Rename preset · Delete preset). The tables below are the keys of the three built-in presets.
- Find an action with **Search actions**, press **Record new shortcut**, then press the key (<kbd class="k-pc">Esc</kbd><kbd class="k-mac">⎋</kbd> to cancel). **Remove shortcut** · **Reset to default** · **Reset all**.
- If another action uses the same key, it is highlighted to tell you. A key you set yourself wins over the preset's default.
- **Touch shortcut**: actions for finger taps and holds. → [Touch and pen](/en/touch-pen)

### Bringing in another app's shortcuts

Pick the shortcut file of the app you used with **Open File**, or drop it onto the window, and its keys come in as a **new preset** (you are told how many keys had no matching action).

| App | File |
|---|---|
| CLIP STUDIO PAINT | Shortcut settings `.khc` · tool palette `.todb` (pick both together for one preset) |
| TVPaint | `config.ini` · keymap `.cfg` · a `.txt` saved from the shortcut window |

## Good to know

- **On the X-sheet, the directions follow the sheet.** Switch the timeline to the X-sheet (vertical) with **⇄** at the right end of its top bar, and the up and down keys go to the previous and next block, left and right to the layer below and above, and Shift with up and down to the previous and next frame.
- **Play, To Start, [Edit](/en/timeline?id=edit) and the comma keys press the button of the panel you touched last.** On the [Conte](/en/storyboard) panel, Play plays the whole track, To Start goes to the track's start, Edit opens the window for the block under the cursor (the cut's name, for a cut), and the comma keys change that block's length (the cut's length, for a cut block).
- **The comma keys** (Set 1–4 Commas, Set N Commas…) are the same as the **1 2 3 4 N** buttons at the top of the timeline and the Conte panel. They change the length of every selected block (the current block when nothing is selected), and the blocks after them close up or move along by that much — select all of `1--2--3--`, set 1 comma, and it becomes `123`.
