## Good to know

- **On the X-sheet the directions follow the sheet.** Switch the timeline to the X-sheet (vertical) with **⇄** at the right end of its top bar, and the up and down keys go to the previous and next block, left and right to the layer below and above, and Shift with up and down to the previous and next frame.
- **Play, To Start, [Edit](/en/timeline?id=the-edit-button) and the comma keys press the button of the panel you touched last.** On the [conte](/en/storyboard) panel, Play plays the whole track, To Start goes to the track's start, Edit opens the window for the block under the cursor (a cut's name, for a cut), and the comma keys set that block's length (the cut's length, for a cut block).
- **The comma keys** (Set 1–4 Commas, Set N Commas…) are the **1 2 3 4 N** buttons at the top of the timeline and conte panels. They set the length of every selected block (the current block when nothing is selected), and the blocks after it close up or move along — select all of `1--2--3--`, set 1 comma, and it reads `123`.
- **Every key can be changed.** **Settings** (gear) at the top left → **Keyboard shortcuts…** lists them all. A key you set yourself wins over the default.
