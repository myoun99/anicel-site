# Send Feedback

Send bugs, features you want and anything you notice while working, straight from the app.

## How to send

1. At the bottom of the **Settings** (⚙) menu, under **About Anicel**, press **Send Feedback**.
2. In the window, choose one of the two.

| Button | |
|---|---|
| **Text only** | Sends text alone. No sign-in. |
| **With screenshots / videos (Google sign-in)** | Attaches images and videos, such as screenshots and screen recordings. You sign in to Google. |

3. A Google Form opens in your browser with **the device, the OS, its version and the app's build** already filled in. Write your message and submit it.

- The form is in English.
- Only images and videos can be attached.
- No other device details, such as the computer's name or your user name, are filled in.

## When an earlier file does not open

If a newer version cannot open one of your earlier files, upload it to a file transfer service such as GigaFile, then write **its link** and **the email address the converted file should go to** in **Send Feedback**. We will convert it to the new format and send it back.
