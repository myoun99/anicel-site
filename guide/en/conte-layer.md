# Storyboard layer

The layer you draw a cut's conte on. Each cut has **one** (named **Conte**; its color label starts as **Storyboard Material**).

![](img/conte-layer.png)

## Making one

- **Layer ＋ ▾ → Storyboard** on the timeline.
- On the [Conte panel](/en/storyboard), press **＋** in the **Conte** row over a cut that has no storyboard layer.
- To turn an animation layer you already drew into a storyboard layer: select that layer and check **Storyboard layer** in the **Layer** pill's menu. Uncheck it and it becomes an animation layer again (as a storyboard layer, its empty cells get filled in).
- If the cut already has a storyboard layer, you can't make another.

## Splitting into panels

- **One block is one panel of the [conte sheet](/en/sheets)**.
- There are no empty cells. A block runs up to the next block or the end of the cut, so there is no × (Clear), hold or repeat.
- Press **Frame ＋** inside a block to split the panel there. Drag the border between blocks and the panels on either side trade length.
- Draw on the canvas, or straight onto the [Conte Sheet](/en/sheets) panel.

## What the whole track shares

The cuts on one track share their storyboard layer's **name · color label · opacity · blend · effect set-up · attach set-up**. Change it in one cut and every cut changes. Panels and drawings are each cut's own.

## Layers attached to the conte

You can attach [attach layers](/en/attach-layers) to a storyboard layer too. Attach layers on the conte can't be detached.

## Export

- Export it as PDF (vector), PNG or JPG from [Export](/en/export) → **Conte Sheet**.
- To watch the conte as a movie, play it on the [Conte panel](/en/storyboard) or export it as a sequence.
