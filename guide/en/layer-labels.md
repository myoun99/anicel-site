# Color labels and takes

Give each layer its **stage** (storyboard · layout · key · inbetween …), and its row and blocks are tinted in that stage's color. You can tell at a glance on the timeline which step each drawing is at.

![](img/labels.png)

## Adding a label

Press the **color label** cell at the far left of a row and pick a stage.

| Stage | On the chip | Revisions you can pick |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | PAP | (none) |
| Storyboard | SB | Director · Chief Director |
| Art | ART | Direction · Animation Director · Chief Animation Director · Director · Chief Director · Action Animation Director |
| Layout | LO | 〃 |
| Rough Key | RK | 〃 |
| Key | KEY | 〃 |
| Inbetween | IB | the six above + **Inbetween Check** |
| Finish | FIN | the six above + **Cell Check** |

- When you pick a stage, its **Material** (that stage's working version) and **revision** steps open beside it. The chip shows the two together, as in "LO AD".
- Clear it with **No label**.
- New layers are born with a label by kind: animation = Layout Material, storyboard = Storyboard Material, image = Art Material.
- [Attach layers](/en/attach-layers) inherit the base layer's label.

## Cut labels

You can label a whole cut too: **Cut** pill menu at the top of the timeline → **Layer mark**. [Linked cuts](/en/linked-cuts) share the same label.

## Takes

The cell to the right of the label is the **Take** (T1–T9, T1 at first). Raise the number when you redraw the same stage.

## Where labels are used

- **Solo color**: the color label cell on the rail's head row → keeps only the rows of the stage you pick (undo it with **Clear mark filter**).
- **Export**: in [Export](/en/export) → **Cels**, choose which rows to export by label and take (Latest / Take n).
- **Staff**: in **Settings** → **Work settings**, and in a cut's **Cut settings**, write who is in charge of each stage. → [Timesheet, conte sheet, cut envelope](/en/sheets)
