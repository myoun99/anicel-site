# Conte panel

The panel that **lines up the cuts** of the whole work. If the timeline is the inside of one cut, the Conte panel is the outside that joins cut to cut. It plays the cuts in a row even when their sizes differ. Switch to it with the tabs at the bottom of the timeline panel.

![](img/storyboard.png)

## The rows of the Conte panel

| Row | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Camera** (the camera row) | Each cut's camera keys and moves (PAN · T.U · T.B) |
| **Transition** (the transition row) | Scene changes such as FI · FO · O.L |
| **Video** (the video track) | One block is **one cut** |
| **Conte** (the storyboard) | Each cut's [storyboard layer](/en/conte-layer). One cell is one panel of the [conte sheet](/en/sheets) |
| **S1 · S2 …** | [SE](/en/sound-layers) — a sound that runs across cuts is one block too |

## Working with cuts

- On the **Video** row, select cuts, drag them to change the order, and drag their ends to change their length.
- A new cut is the **＋** of the **Cut** pill; duplicate and delete are **Duplicate** · **Delete** in the **Shared** pill.
- Double-click a cut (or use **Edit**) to rename it.
- [Linked cuts](/en/linked-cuts) have 🔗 beside the cut name.

## Drawing the conte

- On the **Conte** row, make a storyboard layer for a cut that has none with **＋**.
- Select panels and change their length; double-click a block to rename the panel. A panel with no drawing yet shows faintly.
- Draw on the canvas or in the [Conte Sheet](/en/sheets) panel.

## O.L (overlap)

- O.L spans two cuts, so you edit it here on the **Transition** row, not on the timeline. Set the kind with **Edit** and the length by dragging the block's end.
- During an O.L, the camera row shows split in two, for the cut before and the cut after.

## Playback

Press **▶** on the Conte panel to play **all the cuts in a row** (on the timeline, only the current cut). → [Playback and recording](/en/playback)

## Good to know

- There is one video track.
- Drag rows on the rail to change their order (the CAM section's order is fixed).
- The solo and show menus are the same as on the timeline. → [Layers at a glance](/en/layers)
